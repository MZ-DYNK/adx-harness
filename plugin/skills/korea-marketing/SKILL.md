# Korea Marketing

Treat marketing as one connected system: business objective, customer, value,
message, journey, channel, experience, and measurement.

## Start with the decision

1. Read `.harness/MARKETING.md` when it exists.
2. Define the business result and time horizon.
3. Name the priority audience and behavior to change.
4. Identify the customer tension, alternative, or unmet job.
5. Set the value proposition and positioning before producing channel assets.
6. Map the journey across paid, owned, earned, commerce, and CRM touchpoints.
7. Choose channels based on the audience and job, not a default media list.
8. Define leading, outcome, and guardrail metrics.

Read [references/integrated-playbook.md](references/integrated-playbook.md) for
strategy, brand, advertising, digital, and channel planning. Read
[references/crm-and-measurement.md](references/crm-and-measurement.md) only when
the strategy needs lifecycle or measurement architecture. Use
`audit-marketing-report`, `plan-marketing-experiment`,
`design-crm-lifecycle`, or `reconcile-marketing-decision` for their narrower
execution workflows.

## Apply Korean market context

Read [references/korea-market.md](references/korea-market.md) for channel,
commerce, cultural, and compliance research prompts.

Platform products, media specifications, prices, consumer statistics, laws,
policies, and consent requirements change. Verify them from current primary
sources, record the verification date, and separate confirmed fact from
inference. Do not present marketing guidance as legal advice.

## Work modes

### Strategy or go-to-market

Deliver:

- objective and constraint
- market and customer insight
- segmentation, target, and position
- offer and message hierarchy
- channel and journey logic
- measurement plan
- risks and open decisions

### Campaign or advertising

Lock the audience, behavioral objective, proposition, support, tone, mandatories,
channel role, and response mechanism before writing creative. Give each asset one
job in the campaign sequence.

### Research

Use read-only subagents when there are independent market, competitor, customer,
or channel axes. Require `finding / evidence / implication / uncertainty`, then
integrate the decision in the main thread.

## Output rules

- Give a recommended direction before a menu of tactics.
- Tie each tactic to audience, journey stage, and metric.
- Distinguish brand-building, demand capture, conversion, and retention jobs.
- Include a downside case or counter-evidence for meaningful investments.
- Label assumptions and estimates.
- Update `.harness/MARKETING.md` only with durable project learning, accepted
  positioning, or measured results.

## Quality gate

- The recommendation follows from the stated objective and customer insight.
- Korean channel choices are audience-specific and current.
- CRM includes eligibility, suppression, frequency, and consent considerations.
- Metrics connect to business outcomes rather than vanity activity alone.
- Performance decisions state the data contract, evidence level, and action
  threshold; efficient but tiny samples are not presented as scalable winners.
- Claims, dates, and sources are inspectable.
